01 Remote

A private line to your host.

Mimi Remote rides your own Tailscale network — a WireGuard tunnel from the device in your hand straight to the Mac or Windows PC doing the work. No relay in the middle, no port forwarding, nothing published to the open internet.

  • Direct and encrypted

    Peer-to-peer WireGuard between your own devices. Keys never leave them, and no third party sits in the path.

  • Recovers on its own

    Drop the signal and nothing is lost. Unsent instructions wait in a queue on the device and go out the moment the link returns.

  • Works wherever you are

    Café Wi-Fi, cellular, a locked-down office network — if both ends are on your tailnet, the session is right there.

02 iPhone & iPad

Two screens. Two layouts.

Not one interface stretched to fit. The iPad gets a standing sidebar with in-progress work and recent history in view; the iPhone gets a single column and a tab bar your thumb can reach.

03 Runtimes

Codex and Claude Code, side by side.

Both runtimes are first-class. Pick one when you start a session, and switch which you are looking at without leaving the screen.

Codex Claude Code

Sessions

Every conversation across every project, grouped by when it happened and searchable in one field. Work still running is marked as running.

Workspaces

Each repository is a tab of its own, carrying its recent conversations and the branch each one ran on. Starting a new session there takes one tap.

04 Personal
