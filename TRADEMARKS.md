# Mimi Remote 商标与品牌使用政策 / Trademark and Brand Policy

生效日期 / Effective date：2026-08-10

## 中文

### 目标

Mimi Remote 保持代码开放，同时让使用者能够判断软件和服务是否来自官方维护者。本政策只说明项目名称、视觉标识和官方发行身份的使用边界，不改变任何开源许可证授予的代码权利。

本政策不声称任何项目标识已经注册为商标。它说明项目维护者允许第三方如何使用这些标识，以及如何避免对来源、背书或官方身份造成混淆。

### 方案

本政策中的“项目标识”包括：

- `Mimi Remote`、`咪咪 Remote`、`MimiRemote` 和 `mimi-remote`，仅限它们被用于识别产品、发行版或服务时。
- Mimi Remote 的官方 Logo、App 图标、组合标识及其他能够识别官方发行来源的视觉元素。
- 当项目标识被用于 App 名称、商店页面、域名、社交账号、软件包身份或发行渠道时，本政策同样适用，尤其是在该使用容易让用户误认为相关渠道由官方维护者控制的情况下。

当前版本中由 Mimi Remote 权利人拥有的源代码、二进制和文档，其复制、修改与分发继续由 [LICENSE](LICENSE) 管理；第三方组件和此前已经明确按 MIT License 发布的历史版本继续适用各自原有许可证。本政策不减少这些权利；如果本政策与适用的开源许可证在代码权利上冲突，以该开源许可证为准。Fork 复制或修改本政策文件，不会使第三方取得代表项目维护者变更品牌授权的权利。

### 实现

#### 无需书面授权

在不造成来源或背书混淆的前提下，你可以：

- 保留适用许可证要求的版权、许可、修改和上游归属声明；为准确说明来源，可以在这些声明中使用项目名称。这不要求把项目名称继续作为面向用户的品牌。
- 在文字中如实说明软件“基于 Mimi Remote”“与 Mimi Remote 兼容”或来源于本仓库，并链接官方仓库。
- 在新闻、评论、研究、Issue、PR 和社区讨论中指称 Mimi Remote。
- 使用自己的主要品牌提供独立的兼容、培训或支持服务，并在相关说明旁明确标注“独立提供，与 Mimi Remote 官方维护者无隶属或背书关系”。
- 重新分发未经修改的官方 Release，但必须遵守适用许可证、标明原始来源，并清楚说明该下载渠道由第三方运营而非官方发行渠道。

上述允许不包含把项目标识注册为自己的商标、域名、账号或软件身份，也不包含使用官方 Logo 或 App 图标作为第三方产品的主要标识。

#### 需要事先书面授权

以下使用需要项目维护者事先书面授权：

- 让修改版、Fork、衍生产品或服务继续以 Mimi Remote 作为面向用户的产品名称、主要品牌、Logo 或 App 图标。
- 在 App Store、软件包仓库、域名、社交账号、开发者名称或发行者名称中使用项目标识，并可能让用户误认为它是官方渠道。
- 使用 `Mimi Remote Pro`、`Mimi Remote Cloud` 等容易被理解为官方版本、官方扩展或官方服务的名称。
- 在广告、合作声明、商品或销售材料中暗示项目维护者提供了认证、赞助、合作或质量保证。

#### 修改版和 Fork

以下要求适用于公开分发的、面向用户的修改版 App 或二进制发行包。仅保存或发布源码的 Fork 和内部构建，不因本政策被要求更改 App 或软件包身份；但如果它们作为独立产品对外宣传，仍不得使用项目标识作为主要品牌。

- 使用清晰独立的主要名称、Logo 和 App 图标。
- 在相关字段实际存在且可能造成来源混淆时，使用自己控制的 App 显示名称、Bundle ID 或软件包 ID、签名身份和更新渠道。
- 不要在产品标题、商店标题、发行者名称、域名或账号名称中把项目标识作为主要品牌。
- 可以在描述中如实写明“基于 Mimi Remote”，但应在相邻位置明确说明它是独立项目，不由 Mimi Remote 官方维护者发布、认可或支持。
- 继续保留适用许可证要求的版权、许可、修改和第三方归属声明。重新命名不会改变适用于相关 GPL 覆盖作品的 GPLv3 义务。

没有面向用户展示、也不会造成来源混淆的内部代码标识不要求仅为品牌原因重命名。

#### 官方发行身份

只有由 Mimi Remote 官方维护者控制、并从[规范仓库](https://github.com/gaixianggeng/mimi-remote)链接的仓库、商店页面和账号才是官方渠道；只有通过这些渠道发布或签名的发行包才是官方 Release。第三方镜像可以如实说明其提供的是未经修改的官方 Release，但不得把自己的下载渠道、产品或支持服务描述为官方渠道或官方服务。

#### 第三方标识

`Codex`、`OpenAI`、`ChatGPT`、`Claude`、`Anthropic`、`GitHub`、`Tailscale` 以及仓库中出现的其他第三方名称和 Logo 归各自权利人所有。本政策不代表这些权利人，也不授予其标识的任何使用权。

### 风险与优化

适用法律允许的合理指称、评论和其他法定使用不受本政策限制。如果你不确定计划中的名称、商店页面或宣传材料是否会造成混淆，请在发布前联系 `gaixg94@gmail.com`。

本政策可以随项目品牌和发行方式更新，但更新不会追溯改变已经授予的开源代码权利或单独签署的书面授权。

## English

### Purpose

Mimi Remote keeps its code open while helping people identify whether software or services come from the official maintainers. This policy governs only project names, visual identity, and official-distribution indicators. It does not change any code rights granted by an open-source license.

This policy does not claim that any Project Mark is registered. It explains how the project maintainer permits third parties to use these identifiers and how to avoid confusion about source, endorsement, or official status.

### Approach

“Project Marks” in this policy include:

- `Mimi Remote`, `咪咪 Remote`, `MimiRemote`, and `mimi-remote` when used to identify a product, distribution, or service.
- Mimi Remote's official logo, app icon, combined marks, and other visual elements that identify an official distribution source.
- This policy also applies when Project Marks are used in app names, store listings, domains, social accounts, package identities, or distribution channels, especially where that use could make people reasonably believe the channel is controlled by the official maintainers.

Copying, modification, and distribution of Mimi Remote-owned source code, binaries, and documentation in the current version remain governed by [LICENSE](LICENSE). Third-party components and historical versions previously and explicitly released under the MIT License remain governed by their original licenses. This policy does not reduce those rights. If it conflicts with an applicable open-source license about rights in code, that open-source license controls. Copying or modifying this policy in a fork does not give a third party authority to change brand permissions on behalf of the project maintainer.

### Implementation

#### No written permission required

As long as you do not create confusion about source or endorsement, you may:

- Keep the copyright, license, modification, and upstream-attribution notices required by the applicable licenses. You may use the project name in those notices to identify the source accurately; this does not require keeping the project name as the user-facing brand.
- Truthfully state in text that software is “based on Mimi Remote,” “compatible with Mimi Remote,” or derived from this repository, and link to the official repository.
- Refer to Mimi Remote in news, reviews, research, issues, pull requests, and community discussion.
- Offer independent compatibility, training, or support services under your own primary brand, with a nearby statement that the service is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the official Mimi Remote maintainers.
- Redistribute an unmodified official release, provided you comply with the applicable licenses, identify the original source, and clearly state that the download channel is operated by a third party rather than being an official distribution channel.

These permissions do not include registering Project Marks as your own trademarks, domains, accounts, or software identities, or using the official logo or app icon as the primary identity of a third-party product.

#### Prior written permission required

You need prior written permission from the project maintainer to:

- Use Mimi Remote as the user-facing product name, primary brand, logo, or app icon of a modified version, fork, derivative product, or service.
- Use Project Marks in an App Store listing, package repository, domain, social account, developer name, or publisher name in a way that may appear to be an official channel.
- Use names such as `Mimi Remote Pro` or `Mimi Remote Cloud` that may be understood as an official version, extension, or service.
- Suggest in advertising, partnership statements, merchandise, or sales materials that the project maintainer provides certification, sponsorship, partnership, or a quality guarantee.

#### Modified versions and forks

The following requirements apply when publicly distributing a user-facing modified app or binary release. A source-only fork or internal build does not need a new app or package identity solely because of this policy. If it is promoted as an independent product, however, it still must not use a Project Mark as its primary brand.

- Choose a clearly distinct primary name, logo, and app icon.
- Where those fields exist and may cause source confusion, use an app display name, Bundle ID or package ID, signing identity, and update channel that you control.
- Do not use a Project Mark as the primary brand in a product title, store title, publisher name, domain, or account name.
- You may truthfully say “based on Mimi Remote” in the description, but place a nearby statement that it is an independent project and is not published, endorsed, or supported by the official Mimi Remote maintainers.
- Keep all copyright, license, modification, and third-party attribution notices required by the applicable licenses. Rebranding does not change the GPLv3 obligations that apply to a relevant GPL-covered work.

Internal code identifiers that are not user-facing and cannot create confusion about source do not need to be renamed solely for branding reasons.

#### Official distribution identity

Only repositories, store listings, and accounts controlled by the official Mimi Remote maintainers and linked from the [canonical repository](https://github.com/gaixianggeng/mimi-remote) are official channels. Only release packages published or signed through those channels are official releases. A third-party mirror may truthfully state that it provides an unmodified official release, but it must not describe its own download channel, product, or support service as official.

#### Third-party marks

`Codex`, `OpenAI`, `ChatGPT`, `Claude`, `Anthropic`, `GitHub`, `Tailscale`, and other third-party names and logos present in the repository belong to their respective owners. This policy does not represent those owners or grant any rights to their marks.

### Risks and future changes

Nothing in this policy limits truthful referential use, commentary, or other uses allowed by applicable law. If you are unsure whether a planned name, store listing, or promotional material may cause confusion, contact `gaixg94@gmail.com` before release.

This policy may be updated as the project's branding and distribution change. Updates do not retroactively alter open-source code rights already granted or separate written permissions already issued.
